# Pitch Guide — 7 Wonders Studio

## The one-line pitch
> "Right now, when someone in Bandra searches for a salon, they find Justdial pages that list you next to Lakmé and Jean-Claude Biguine. A website of your own puts you first, makes you look as premium as you are, and lets people book you on WhatsApp in ten seconds."

## Before the meeting
1. Deploy the demo to a live link (Vercel / Netlify / GitHub Pages) so you can open it on **their phone**, not your laptop. Owners judge on mobile.
2. Swap 3–4 stock photos for real photos from their Instagram (screenshot or ask permission). Seeing their own studio on the site is the moment that sells it.
3. Put their real phone number in the WhatsApp booking so the demo form actually messages them.
4. Google "unisex salon Bandra West" and "salon Hill Road Bandra" — screenshot where they appear (or don't). That's your problem statement.

## Meeting flow (15 minutes)
1. **Problem (2 min)** — Show the Google search screenshot. Competitors on the same road have proper websites; 7 Wonders depends on Instagram and listing sites.
2. **Demo (5 min)** — Open the live link on their phone. Walk through:
   - The giant "7 / WONDERS" wordmark — premium first impression.
   - "The Seven Wonders" — their name becomes their menu. Easy to remember and talk about.
   - Book a visit → it opens WhatsApp with the request already typed. No new software for staff to learn.
   - Get directions → one tap to Google Maps.
3. **What the full site adds (3 min)** — prices, real gallery, team, Google reviews, bridal page, SEO to rank in Bandra.
4. **Offer & next step (5 min)** — packages below, timeline (about 6 weeks), and the content you need from them.

## Value points that land with salon owners
- **More bookings, fewer DMs** — pre-filled WhatsApp messages instead of "price?" DMs.
- **Bridal season** — a bridal page and enquiry form captures the highest-value clients.
- **Owns their brand** — Instagram can change its algorithm; the website and Google ranking are theirs.
- **Looks as good as the big chains** on the same street, without franchise budgets.
- **Easy to update** — they change prices and offers themselves.

## Suggested packages (adjust to your rates)
| Package | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Essential** | Polished single-page site (this design), WhatsApp booking, Google Maps, basic SEO, domain + hosting setup |
| **Signature** (recommended) | 5–6 pages, services & prices, gallery, team, Google reviews, bridal enquiry, CMS so they can edit, analytics, local SEO |
| **Signature + Care** | Everything above + monthly retainer: offers updates, new photos, SEO reporting, seasonal campaign pages |

Tip: anchor with Signature. Offer a launch discount if they sign before wedding season.

## Likely objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Instagram is enough." | Instagram is great for discovery, but Google is where people search "salon near me". This site connects both: every Instagram visitor gets a clean place to book. |
| "It's expensive." | One extra bridal booking or a handful of new regulars a month pays for it. Then it keeps working. |
| "We don't have time to manage it." | You don't have to. The care plan covers updates; or you change prices yourself in two minutes. |
| "We already get listings on Justdial." | Those pages also advertise your competitors to your customers. Your site advertises only you. |

## Extra ideas you can float
- **"7th visit on us"** loyalty card — ties directly to the name.
- Google Business Profile clean-up (photos, hours, review replies) as an add-on.
- QR code at the reception desk that opens the booking page and the Google review link.
- Before/after Reels embedded on service pages.
